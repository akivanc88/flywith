How it works
Smart routing in 3 steps
FlyWith scores layover cities based on your travel profile, not just connection time.
1

Tell us your trip

Origin, destination, dates, and who's traveling — solo, with kids, elderly parents, or on a tight budget.

2

We score every layover city

Our algorithm rates each potential stopover on ease, family-friendliness, senior accessibility, visa requirements, and entertainment value.

3

You pick a mini-vacation

See real fares, total cost comparison, and curated things to do — then book through us or jump to Google Flights / Skyscanner if we can't beat their price.

4

Arrive refreshed

No more zombie-walking through arrivals. You've already seen Dubai, Amsterdam, or Tokyo — and spent less than the "fast" route.
